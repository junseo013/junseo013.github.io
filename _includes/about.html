I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Seoul National University, where I am advised by Prof. Jaewoong Sim in the Computer Architecture and Systems Lab.

My research focuses on computer architecture, with an emphasis on architectural support for emerging workloads. In particular, I study how to efficiently execute emerging visual computing workloads, such as neural rendering, 3D Gaussian splatting, and ray tracing, through designs ranging from domain-specific accelerators to extensions of the hardware graphics pipeline in GPUs. My work has been published at top-tier computer architecture conferences, such as ISCA, ASPLOS, and HPCA.

I received my B.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Seoul National University in 2022.
